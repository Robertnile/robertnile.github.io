About

From the queue to the write-up

I completed a year-long SOC Analyst Traineeship at Luke Tech Limited (Canada, remote), working from Nigeria. My background is self-directed: every lab on this page started as "how would I actually detect this?" and ended with a working detection pipeline, SPL or VQL queries, and a report I'd hand to a hiring manager.

I care about understanding why a detection works, not just copying a query that does. That means reading the protocol spec before writing the Zeek signature, and debugging the VQL aggregate function until I understand what it's doing under the hood.

My latest lab is a full static and dynamic malware analysis of an AgentTesla JavaScript dropper — C2 extraction on REMnux, WMI execution chain tracing, and sandbox validation on Hybrid Analysis and VirusTotal scoring 100/100.
